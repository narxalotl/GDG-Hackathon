Virginia Residential Landlord and Tenant Act (VRLTA) - Security Deposit Rules
Source: Virginia Code § 55.1-1226

1. Statutory Return Deadline (§ 55.1-1226(A)):
   - Landlords must return the full security deposit, or the balance after lawful deductions, within 45 days after the termination of tenancy or the date the tenant vacates, whichever occurs last.
   - Landlords must provide a written, itemized accounting listing all deductions made.

2. Permitted Deductions:
   - Unpaid rent and late fees specified in the lease.
   - Actual physical damage caused by tenant noncompliance or negligence.
   - Landlords CANNOT deduct for normal wear and tear (e.g., minor carpet wear, small scuffs).

3. Tenant Rights & Dispute Process:
   - Tenants have the right to inspect the property with the landlord present within 72 hours of moving out.
   - Failure to provide an itemized statement within 45 days may result in forfeiture of the right to withhold any portion of the deposit, plus actual damages and attorney fees under § 55.1-1226(E).
